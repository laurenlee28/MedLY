    How to Use MedLY

    Steps:

    1. Click the Start button to begin recording the conversation.
    2. Speak clearly. The transcribed text will appear in the Diagnosis area.
    3. Once you are finished, click the Stop button.
    4. Wait for the Summary to be generated. This may take a moment.
    5. Key medical terms identified in the conversation will appear under Key Terms. These terms will gently pulse to indicate they are interactive.
    6. Click on a Key Term. A brief explanation of the term will appear in the Definition area on the right.
    7. You can change the reading level of the definition (Child, Student, Adult) by clicking the corresponding buttons.
    8. Click the New Session button (which replaces the Stop button) to start a new recording and analysis.
    9. You can download the conversation transcript, key terms, and summary as a PDF by clicking the Download PDF button.

    Features:

    • Real-time Transcription: Converts spoken language into text.
    • Key Term Identification: Automatically identifies important medical terms.
    • Term Definitions: Provides explanations of medical terms at different reading levels.
    • Summary Generation: Creates a concise summary of the medical conversation.
    • PDF Export: Allows you to download a report of the session.
